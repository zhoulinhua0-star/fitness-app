## English

RepDay is a focused workout planner and tracker for iPhone. Build a repeatable
weekly schedule, train from a plan or improvise when the day changes, record every
set and rep, and choose the right rest time for each exercise.

### What you can do

- Plan workouts across your week and reuse saved templates.
- Start either a planned workout or an improvised training session.
- Track exercises, sets, reps, completion, and workout history.
- Set a different rest duration for each exercise.
- Review training statistics and recent progress.
- Optionally sync your schedule to Apple Calendar.
- Use local reminders and the RepDay home-screen widget.

RepDay does not require an account. Workout data is stored locally on your iPhone,
with no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The app interface is currently
available in Simplified Chinese.

## 简体中文

RepDay 是一款专注于训练计划和健身记录的 iPhone 应用。你可以建立每周训练课表，
按照计划开始训练，也可以在计划有变时进入即兴模式；每一组、每一次重复和每段休息
时间都能清晰记录。

### 核心功能

- 创建每周训练计划，并通过模板快速复用训练内容。
- 在计划训练和即兴训练之间自由选择。
- 记录动作、组数、次数、完成进度和历史训练。
- 为不同动作分别设置合适的组间休息时间。
- 查看训练统计和近期进度。
- 可选同步训练计划到 Apple 日历。
- 支持本地提醒和 RepDay 主屏幕小组件。

RepDay 无需注册账户。训练数据保存在你的 iPhone 本地，不包含广告、分析或追踪
SDK。应用界面目前为简体中文。
