**Description**

Train with a clear plan and keep every workout organized.

RepDay is a focused workout planner and tracker for iPhone. Build a weekly schedule, start an improvised session when plans change, record your sets and reps, and choose a rest time that fits each exercise.

FEATURES

• Plan workouts across your week  
• Create and reuse workout templates  
• Start planned or improvised training sessions  
• Track exercises, sets, reps, and workout history  
• Set different rest times for different exercises  
• Review training statistics and recent progress  
• Optionally sync your workout schedule to Apple Calendar  
• Use local reminders and the RepDay home-screen widget

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

RepDay does not require an account. Your workout data is stored locally on your iPhone. The app does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Calendar access and notifications are optional and can be changed at any time in iPhone Settings.

## App Review Information

**Sign-in required**  
No. Do not provide a demo account.

**Review Notes**

RepDay is a local-only workout planning and logging app. No account or sign-in is required. Workout plans, logs, and history are stored on the user's device.

Calendar access is optional and is requested only when the user chooses Sync to Calendar. Notifications are optional and are used only for local workout reminders and rest timers. The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or a remote notification server.

The app's interface is currently in Simplified Chinese. The main tabs are: Today (今日), Statistics (统计), Plan (计划), and Profile (我的).

To test the core flow: open Plan (计划), choose a training day, add or edit exercises, return to Today (今日), and complete sets. Calendar and notification permissions may be denied without blocking the core workout flow.

**Attachment**  
None required unless App Review asks for a demonstration video.
